Our story

From a small workshop to your backyard

Founded in 2018 by a group of designers and craftspeople, PatioNest began with a simple belief: outdoor spaces deserve the same attention to detail as indoor ones.

Today, we work with local artisans and sustainable suppliers to bring you furniture that’s both beautiful and built to last. Every piece is a testament to our commitment to quality, comfort, and the environment.

Our Journey

Key milestones that shaped our commitment to outdoor living.

2018

Artisanal Workshop

Founded in a small coastal studio with two master carpenters dedicated to 100% FSC-certified teak.

2020

First Signature Line

Launched our weather-resistant garden chair series. Entire debut inventory sold out in 3 weeks.

2022

B2B & Resort Furnishing

Expanded into commercial boutique hotels, beach resorts & architectural outdoor projects.

2026

Global Living Brand

Now delivering artisan patio furniture to homeowners across 15+ countries with carbon-neutral shipping.
